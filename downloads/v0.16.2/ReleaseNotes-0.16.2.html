DeskNest 0.16.2

简体中文

更新通道：stable；构建版本：0.16.2.65535；Windows x64。

  • 补齐设置中心及相关对话框的英文翻译，并新增漏译回归检查。
  • 修复设置中心在最小窗口宽度下英文内容被裁切的问题。
  • 更新说明、更新弹窗和便携版更新器会跟随所选界面语言显示中文或英文。
  • 改进 Folder Portal 的返回、前进和上级按钮反馈。
  • 保持现有安全更新、桌面文件和 Box 外观行为不变。

English

Channel: stable; build: 0.16.2.65535; Windows x64.

  • Completed English translations for Settings and related dialogs with regression checks.
  • Fixed clipped English content when Settings is at its minimum window width.
  • Localized update notes, update dialogs, and the portable updater to the selected language.
  • Improved feedback for Folder Portal back, forward, and up controls.
  • Kept existing safe update, desktop-file, and Box-appearance behavior unchanged.
